## 1. Company Overview

Forever Living Products International is the world's largest grower,
manufacturer, and distributor of aloe vera. Founded in 1978 in Scottsdale,
Arizona (USA) by Rex Maughan, the company sells aloe-vera-based drinks,
nutritional supplements, bee products, and personal care items through a
direct-selling / multi-level-marketing model.

**Forever Living Products Kenya** was established in **2005** and is based in
**Nairobi**. The Kenya office also serves as the East Africa hub, supporting
operations in **Ethiopia, Somalia, South Sudan, Rwanda, and Uganda**.

## 3. Product Lines Available in Kenya

The Kenya retail store organizes products into these main categories:

- **Drinks** — the flagship line, e.g. Forever Aloe Vera Gel (99.7% pure
  inner-leaf aloe gel; sugar-free, gluten-free, no added preservatives),
  plus flavored variants (Berry Nectar, Peaches, etc.).
- **Nutritional supplements** — joint health, omega-3, probiotics, vitamins
  and minerals, immune boosters, and kids' supplements.
- **Bee products** — bee pollen, propolis, royal jelly, honey.
- **Personal care** — skin care, aloe-based lotions, gels, and grooming
  products.
- **Combo packs** — bundled starter/consumer packs.

**Indicative pricing:** Forever Aloe Vera (Drinking) Gel retails around
**KSh 3,291** in Kenya. Full, current prices are listed on the
[Kenya retail store](https://shop.foreverliving.com/retail/entry/Shop.do?store=KEN).

## 4. How to Buy

1. **Retail** — buy at full retail price from the online Kenya store or from
   a local FBO/distributor.
2. **Forever Preferred Customer** — register online for free and buy directly
   from the company at a **5% discount** (grows with accumulated purchases).
3. **Forever Business Owner (FBO)** — join as a distributor to buy at
   wholesale prices and earn income from retailing and team building.

## 5. Joining as a Forever Business Owner (FBO)

- Registration is **free** and can be done online through the Kenya store or
  through a sponsoring FBO (you need a sponsor ID; the company can assign one
  if you don't have a contact).
- There is **no purchase obligation** to register, but rank advancement and
  bonuses depend on sales volume (see marketing plan below).
- After registering you get a distributor ID and access to the
  [distributor portal](https://shop.foreverliving.com/distribNew/Login.do?store=KEN)
  for ordering, downline reports, and bonus statements.

## 6. The Marketing (Compensation) Plan

Progress in FLP is measured in **Case Credits (CC)** — a point value assigned
to every product. Accumulating CC within qualifying periods moves you up the
ranks automatically; **ranks are permanent for life** and can be inherited or
transferred to close family.

### Rank ladder

| Rank | Case Credits required | Typical discount/benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Novus Customer | 0 CC | 5% discount, referral cashback |
| Assistant Supervisor | 2 CC | ~15% discount + personal bonuses |
| Supervisor | 10 CC | Higher personal/volume bonuses |
| Assistant Manager | 60 CC | Higher group volume bonuses |
| Manager | 120 CC | Up to ~30% discount, leadership bonuses |

### Earning components

- **Retail profit** — margin between wholesale and retail price.
- **Personal bonuses** — percentage cashback on your own accredited sales.
- **Group volume bonuses** — up to **13%** on sales by Assistant Supervisors,
  Supervisors, and Assistant Managers in your downline.
- **Leadership bonuses** — **2%–6%** on downline Managers' group volumes.
- **Incentives** — Chairman's Bonus (profit sharing), Forever2Drive (car
  plan), global rallies and travel incentives for qualifying leaders.

Official rules are published in the company's
[Business Plan, Company Policies and Procedures (PDF)](https://cdn.foreverliving.com/content/staticpages/pdfs/footer_staticpage_pdf_1581937518166.pdf).

### Realistic expectations

FLP is a direct-selling/MLM business. Income depends entirely on personal and
team sales volume; most registrants earn little or nothing beyond product
discounts. Treat published earnings claims skeptically and review the official
policies before investing time or money.
